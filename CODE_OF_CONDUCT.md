
# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

CodeYantra Technologies is committed to maintaining a
respectful, inclusive, and professional environment for
developers, contributors, researchers, and community members.

We welcome participation regardless of background,
experience, nationality, gender, religion, or identity.

## Expected Behaviour

Community members are expected to:

- Communicate respectfully and professionally.
- Provide constructive feedback.
- Respect different perspectives and experiences.
- Collaborate responsibly.
- Respect intellectual property and software licenses.
- Protect user privacy and confidential information.
- Follow responsible security disclosure practices.

## Unacceptable Behaviour

The following behaviour is not acceptable:

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Threats, abusive language, or intimidation.
- Sharing private information without consent.
- Spam, malicious contributions, or deliberate disruption.
- Impersonation or misleading representation.
- Unauthorized disclosure of confidential information.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to CodeYantra Technologies
community spaces, including repositories, issues, pull
requests, discussions, and official community interactions.

## Reporting

Unacceptable behaviour may be reported privately to:

support@codeyantra.in

Reports will be handled with appropriate discretion
and reviewed fairly.

## Enforcement

CodeYantra Technologies may take appropriate action,
including warnings, content removal, temporary restrictions,
or permanent exclusion from community spaces.

## Our Philosophy

We believe meaningful innovation grows through respect,
collaboration, integrity, and responsible engineering.
